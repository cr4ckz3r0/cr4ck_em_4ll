THE LONG RIDE

A solitary crossing on horseback · anno 1237

Winter is turning. You and your horse must cross the open steppe, reaching each waypoint before the animal gives out. Read the weather, spare your mount's legs, water and graze it, and rest by the fire. Push too hard and you will lose your only companion to the cold.

How to ride
  • Gaits (1–4): Walk rests legs, Trot travels, Canter is quick, Gallop is fastest but burns stamina fast — overexertion hurts health and bond.
  • Care: stand on lush grass and hold Space to graze; by water, hold Space to drink.
  • Camp: press F near trees to gather wood, C to build/light a campfire, then R to rest — restores stamina, rest, warmth and deepens the bond (skips time).
  • Weather: rain and snow chill you and tire the horse; fog and night cut visibility. Plan hops between waypoints around the forecast.
