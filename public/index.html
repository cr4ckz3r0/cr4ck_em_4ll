THE LONG RIDE

A first-person crossing on horseback · anno 1237

Winter is turning. Seen through your own eyes in the saddle, you and your horse must cross the open country, reaching each beacon before the animal gives out. Look around with the mouse — even up at the wide sky — read the weather, spare your mount's legs, water and graze it, and rest by the fire.

How to ride
  • Look: move the mouse to look around (and up at the sky). Click the world to capture the mouse; press Esc to release it.
  • Ride: W/S forward & back, A/D to step sideways, following where you look.
  • Gaits (1–4): Walk rests the legs, Trot travels, Canter is quick, Gallop is fastest but burns stamina — overexertion hurts health and bond.
  • Care: at a pond hold Space to drink; on lush grass hold Space to graze.
  • Camp: F near trees to gather wood, C to light a campfire, R to rest — recovers and deepens the bond.
